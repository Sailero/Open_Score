# Third-party LaTeX files

Original distributions, under the licenses in their headers:

- IEEEtran.cls 1.8b: https://tug.ctan.org/macros/latex/contrib/IEEEtran/IEEEtran.cls
- IEEEtran.bst 1.14: https://tug.ctan.org/macros/latex/contrib/IEEEtran/bibtex/IEEEtran.bst

The surrounding manuscript uses standard IEEE journal typography and dimensions.

Experimental figure assets are shared with the repository report under ../Open-SCORE/outputs/main/had/figures/. They are recorded-result plots, separate from the third-party LaTeX typography files listed above.
