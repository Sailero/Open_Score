# 2. Related Work

**Scalable and population-invariant MARL architectures.** A rich line of work makes MARL policies structurally independent of the number of agents by treating observations as unordered entity sets: Deep Sets pooling [DeepSets], graph networks [MAGIC, G2ANet], and attention-based encoders. Recent representatives include SPECTra [SPECTra], which reduces attention cost with single-agent-query attention and generates mixing-network weights with a set-transformer hypernetwork, and centralized permutation-equivariant policies [CPE] whose complexity is agnostic to agent count; UPDeT and related transformer policies [UPDeT] demonstrate transfer across SMAC unit counts. These architectures provide the representational substrate we build on (our entity encoder follows the observer-query pattern), but the line of work is validated almost exclusively on *cooperative* tasks and stops at the question of *parameter* invariance. It does not address how a team's *decision structure* — its partition into task groups — should respond when the sizes and composition of *both* teams vary, which is the problem our commander solves. Mean-field methods extend competitive team games to very large populations [MF-Yang, MF-MAPPO] by approximating interactions through population statistics; this deliberately erases group-level structure, which we retain as first-class (we use a mean-field-style summary only as auxiliary context).

**Hierarchical MARL and agent grouping.** Hierarchical decompositions into strategic and tactical levels are standard in complex team tasks [FeudalMARL, HSD]. Closest to us, [HRL-Grouping] learns optimal agent *groupings* as high-level options with permutation-invariant embeddings, but in fully cooperative settings with a fixed catalogue of candidate group structures and no adversary; role-based methods [ROMA, RODE] learn emergent roles that are functions of the agent's own trajectory rather than of an opponent's structure. Application-oriented hierarchical systems for multi-vehicle pursuit and team air-to-air engagements [AHDRL, LFMAPPO, Selmonaj] hand-design the grouping layer (leader–follower assignments, per-agent target selection) and evaluate against fixed scripts at fixed or narrowly varied scales. Across this literature the group count is a hyperparameter and the partition is anchored to the ego team; our commander instead derives the number and semantics of groups from a soft clustering of the *opponent*, which is what allows one policy to re-partition itself under arbitrary (N, M) and novel opponent behavior.

**Opponent generalization: populations, leagues, and response oracles.** Training against a diverse population is the established remedy for opponent overfitting: PSRO and its descendants [PSRO, PipelinePSRO, GlobalPSRO, JBR] iteratively expand a strategy population toward low exploitability; league training with dedicated exploiters powered AlphaStar [AlphaStar]; fictitious co-play and partner-design methods pursue the analogous goal in cooperative settings [FCP, UPD]. Full response-oracle machinery is computationally prohibitive at swarm scales, and recent work seeks lightweight substitutes: generative opponent/partner design [UPD], adversarial curricula over environment configurations [AdvWorldModel]. Our SxS curriculum belongs to this lightweight family — parameterized scripts, prioritized snapshots, and a small number of exploiters — but differs in coupling the *opponent* axis with a *scale* axis in a single frontier-driven sampling scheme; we show ablations where removing either axis selectively destroys the corresponding generalization.

**Opponent modeling and in-context adaptation.** Explicit opponent models range from type-based Bayesian reasoning [QOM] to open-ended opponent generation with in-context recognition [OEOM, IOM] and meta-learned strategic probing [MAGE]. These methods operate on the trajectory of an individual opponent in small games. We lift the in-context idea to the *team* level — encoding aggregate engagement statistics of the opposing team into a style embedding — and, crucially, change its consumer: the embedding conditions grouping and force-allocation decisions rather than a single agent's response policy. Our evaluation against freshly trained exploiters follows the exploitability-proxy methodology of this literature.

**Generalization in cooperative MARL and ad-hoc teamwork.** Zero-shot coordination and ad-hoc teamwork study robustness to unseen *partners* [FCP, E3T, UPD]; SMACv2 introduced stochastic unit compositions to force closed-loop policies [SMACv2]. These are cooperative analogues of our opponent axis. On the environment side, GPU-vectorized frameworks (JaxMARL/SMAX [JaxMARL], Gigastep [Gigastep]) make population-scale training tractable; SwarmArena adopts this design and adds the controls the joint problem requires — parameterized reinforcement processes, force-ratio distributions, attribute-parameterized unit types, and a held-out opponent suite — which no existing competitive benchmark provides together.

**Positioning.** In summary: size-invariant architectures give parameter invariance without opponent-responsive structure; hierarchical grouping methods learn partitions anchored to the ego team at fixed scales; population-based training hardens against opponents at fixed sizes; opponent modeling adapts individuals, not team structures. SAGA occupies the intersection — and the dual zero-shot protocol makes the intersection measurable.

# 2. 相关工作

**可扩展与种群不变的 MARL 架构。** 大量工作通过将观测视为无序实体集合，使 MARL 策略在结构上与智能体数量无关：Deep Sets 池化 [DeepSets]、图网络 [MAGIC, G2ANet] 和基于注意力的编码器。近期代表性工作包括 SPECTra [SPECTra]，它通过单智能体查询注意力降低注意力开销，并使用集合 Transformer 超网络生成混合网络权重；集中式置换等变策略 [CPE]，其复杂度与智能体数量无关；UPDeT 及相关 Transformer 策略 [UPDeT] 展示了跨 SMAC 单位数的迁移。这些架构提供了我们构建所依赖的表示基底（我们的实体编码器遵循观测者查询模式），但这条线的工作几乎完全在*合作*任务上验证，且止步于*参数*不变性的问题。它没有解决当*双方*团队的规模和组成都变化时，团队的*决策结构*——其划分为任务组的方式——应该如何响应的问题，而这正是我们的指挥器所解决的问题。平均场方法通过种群统计量近似交互，将竞争性团队博弈扩展到非常大的种群 [MF-Yang, MF-MAPPO]；这有意抹去了组级结构，而我们将其作为一等公民保留（我们仅将平均场式摘要作为辅助上下文使用）。

**层次化 MARL 与智能体分组。** 将策略与战术层次进行层次化分解是复杂团队任务中的标准做法 [FeudalMARL, HSD]。与我们最接近的，[HRL-Grouping] 学习最优智能体*分组*作为高层选项，使用置换不变嵌入，但在完全合作的设定中，使用固定的候选组结构目录，且没有对手；基于角色的方法 [ROMA, RODE] 学习涌现的角色，这些角色是智能体自身轨迹的函数，而非对手结构的函数。面向应用的层次化系统用于多无人机追击和团队空战 [AHDRL, LFMAPPO, Selmonaj] 手工设计分组层（领导者-跟随者分配、逐智能体目标选择），并在固定或窄范围变化的规模下对固定脚本进行评估。在整个文献中，组数是一个超参数，划分锚定于己方团队；我们的指挥器则从*对手*的软聚类中推导出组的数量和语义，这才是使一个策略能够在任意 (N, M) 和新颖对手行为下重新划分自身的关键。

**对手泛化：种群、联赛和响应预言机。** 对抗多样化种群进行训练是解决对手过拟合的成熟方法：PSRO 及其后续 [PSRO, PipelinePSRO, GlobalPSRO, JBR] 迭代扩展策略种群以趋向低可利用性；使用专用 exploiter 的联赛训练驱动了 AlphaStar [AlphaStar]；虚拟合作博弈和伙伴设计方法在合作设定中追求类似目标 [FCP, UPD]。完整的响应预言机机制在集群规模下计算上不可行，近期工作寻求轻量级替代：生成式对手/伙伴设计 [UPD]、基于环境配置的对抗课程 [AdvWorldModel]。我们的 SxS 课程属于这一轻量级家族——参数化脚本、优先快照和少量 exploiter——但不同之处在于将*对手*轴与*规模*轴耦合在单一的前沿驱动采样方案中；我们展示了消融实验，其中移除任一轴会选择性地破坏对应的泛化。

**对手建模与上下文适应。** 显式对手模型的范围从基于类型的贝叶斯推理 [QOM] 到具有上下文识别的开放式对手生成 [OEOM, IOM] 和元学习的策略探测 [MAGE]。这些方法在小型博弈中对单个对手的轨迹进行操作。我们将上下文思想提升到*团队*层面——将对方团队的汇总交战统计数据编码为风格嵌入——并且关键地改变了其消费者：该嵌入条件化的是分组和兵力分配决策，而非单个智能体的响应策略。我们使用新训练的 exploiter 进行评估遵循了这一文献的可利用性代理方法论。

**合作 MARL 与即兴组队中的泛化。** 零样本协调和即兴组队研究对未见*伙伴*的鲁棒性 [FCP, E3T, UPD]；SMACv2 引入随机单位组成以强制闭环策略 [SMACv2]。这些是我们对手轴的合作类比。在环境方面，GPU 向量化框架（JaxMARL/SMAX [JaxMARL]、Gigastep [Gigastep]）使种群规模的训练变得可行；SwarmArena 采用这一设计，并添加了联合问题所需的控制——参数化的增援过程、兵力比分布、属性参数化的单位类型，以及保留对手套件——这是现有竞争性基准未曾一起提供的。

**定位。** 总结：规模不变架构提供了参数不变性但缺乏对手响应式结构；层次化分组方法在固定规模下学习锚定于己方团队的划分；基于种群的训练在固定规模下增强对手鲁棒性；对手建模适应个体而非团队结构。SAGA 占据了交叉点——而双重零样本协议使这一交叉点可以被度量。
